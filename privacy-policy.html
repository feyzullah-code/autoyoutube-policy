Privacy Policy for AutoYouTube AI

Last updated: August 2026

1. Introduction

AutoYouTube AI ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our automated video publishing application.

2. Information We Collect

When you authenticate via TikTok or YouTube, we collect basic account details and OAuth access tokens required to upload content on your behalf.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to automatically upload short-form videos (Shorts/Reels/TikToks) generated by the application to your connected accounts.

4. Data Protection & Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. Your access tokens are stored securely and used only for API authorization.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions regarding this policy, please contact us via our official GitHub repository.
